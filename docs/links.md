## Design Inspiration
- FLAWLESS SKIN CARE
Website Requirements & Functionality Proposal
Prepared for Client Review
1. Project Overview
Flawless Skin Care is a skincare and beauty business based in Limbe, Cameroon, with approximately six years of operation and customers in Cameroon and internationally, including Canada, the USA, Germany and other locations.
The new website will significantly improve the business's previous Wix website. It will provide a modern public website and a secure admin panel that allows the CEO or an authorized manager to update products, images, services, reviews and business information without depending on a developer.
Brand slogan: “The secret for a glowing skin”
2. Main Purpose of the Website
•	Present Flawless Skin Care professionally and build customer trust.
•	Showcase a growing catalogue of skincare products.
•	Allow customers to view detailed product information.
•	Showcase beauty/skincare services and support booking requests.
•	Provide direct WhatsApp communication.
•	Connect visitors to Facebook and TikTok.
•	Display the business location in Limbe, Cameroon and a photo of the shop.
•	Present the CEO as the face of the brand.
•	Display authentic customer reviews/testimonials.
•	Create a strong foundation for Google/search-engine visibility.
•	Give the business owner control over website content through an admin panel.
3. Design & User Experience
The visual direction will take inspiration from premium skincare brands such as Summer Fridays, Glow Recipe, Aesop, Typology, Tatcha and Paula’s Choice, without copying their designs. The result should be an original Flawless Skin Care identity.
•	Premium, modern and elegant.
•	Feminine, warm and approachable.
•	Strong product photography and clean presentation.
•	Generous whitespace and clear visual hierarchy.
•	Editorial-style storytelling where appropriate.
•	Subtle animations rather than excessive effects.
•	Mobile-first and easy to use on phones.
The existing Flawless identity—pink/fuchsia, lilac/lavender, white and subtle gold—will guide the design. These colors will be used strategically rather than making every section bright pink.

5. Admin Panel / Content Management System
The admin panel is a core part of the project. The CEO or authorized manager will have secure access and will be able to update the website without needing a developer.
•	Dashboard.
•	Product management.
•	Category management.
•	Service management.
•	Booking management.
•	Review management.
•	Contact/message management.
•	Brand/CEO profile management.
•	Image/media management.
•	Business settings.
5.1 Product Management
•	Add, edit, delete and publish/unpublish products.
•	Upload multiple product images and select a main image.
•	Replace or remove images.
•	Enter product name, price, descriptions, category and size.
•	Add approved features/benefits.
•	Set availability.
•	Mark featured/favorite/bestseller status.
•	Set SEO title, SEO description and URL slug.
•	Add image alt text.
Products will come from the database, not hardcoded frontend content. The system should remain usable if the catalogue grows to dozens or hundreds of products.
5.2 Service Management
•	Add, edit and delete services.
•	Upload service images.
•	Set description, price and duration.
•	Publish/unpublish services.
5.3 Booking & Message Management
•	View booking requests and customer details.
•	View selected service and requested date/time.
•	Change booking status.
•	View website contact messages.
5.4 Review Management
•	View submitted reviews.
•	Approve or reject reviews.
•	Delete reviews.
•	Control public visibility.
5.5 Brand Profile Management
•	Update CEO name/title.
•	Upload or replace CEO portrait.
•	Update CEO biography.
•	Update brand story.
•	Manage optional quote and social links.
5.6 Business Settings
•	Phone number.
•	WhatsApp number.
•	Email.
•	Address.
•	Opening hours.
•	Facebook URL.
•	TikTok URL.
•	Instagram URL.
•	Other approved business information.
6. Image & Media Management
Because Flawless Skin Care has many products and will continue adding products, the site will include a proper image upload and storage system. The administrator only needs to select and upload an image; storage and delivery happen in the background.
•	Product images.
•	Service images.
•	CEO portrait.
•	Shop photographs.
•	Logo and brand assets.
•	Review/customer images where enabled.
•	Skin Journal images.
Images will be optimized for responsive display, accessibility and performance. Large original phone photographs should not be unnecessarily loaded by every visitor.
7. SEO
SEO will be part of the architecture from the beginning, not an afterthought.
•	Unique page titles and meta descriptions.
•	Search-friendly URLs/slugs.
•	Proper H1/H2/H3 hierarchy.
•	Descriptive image alt text.
•	Open Graph metadata.
•	XML sitemap and robots.txt.
•	Canonical URLs where appropriate.
•	Internal linking.
•	Structured data where appropriate.
•	Fast, mobile-friendly pages.
Potential search themes include Flawless Skin Care, Flawless Skin Care Limbe, skincare in Limbe, skincare products in Limbe, skincare Cameroon and beauty services in Limbe. Keyword stuffing will be avoided.
8. Security
•	Secure admin authentication.
•	Only authorized users can access management features.
•	Public visitors cannot edit products, services or reviews.
•	Customer/private information is protected.
•	Database security rules restrict unauthorized changes.
•	Private credentials are never exposed in frontend code.
9. Technology
•	Next.js + TypeScript.
•	Tailwind CSS.
•	shadcn/ui where useful.
•	Supabase.
•	PostgreSQL through Supabase.
•	Supabase Auth.
•	Supabase Storage for images/media.
•	GitHub for version control.
•	Vercel for deployment.
The technology stack will remain as simple and maintainable as possible while supporting the required functionality and future growth.
10. Customer Journey
DISCOVER → EXPLORE → TRUST → SHOP / BOOK → CONTACT
•	Discover the brand on the homepage.
•	Explore products or services.
•	View product/service details.
•	Build trust through the CEO, brand story and authentic reviews.
•	Contact the business through WhatsApp, phone, email or social media, or submit a booking request.
•	Find the physical shop in Limbe.
11. Admin Journey
LOGIN → DASHBOARD → MANAGE CONTENT → PUBLISH → WEBSITE UPDATES
Example: the manager logs in, adds a product, uploads its image, enters the details and publishes it. The product then appears on the public Shop without changing website code.

13. Information That Will Not Be Invented
The website will not invent product ingredients, medical/clinical claims, certifications, endorsements, customer reviews, exact prices, shipping policies, refund policies, opening hours, contact details or founder information. Missing information will be marked as pending until supplied and approved by the client.
14. Future Expansion

•	Customer accounts.
•	Order management.
•	International shipping workflow.
•	Advanced product recommendations.
•	Skincare quiz/product matching.
•	Analytics.
•	Email/newsletter functionality.

These are future possibilities and are not required for the first launch unless the client approves them.
15. Expected Final Result
The finished website should be a significant improvement over the previous Wix website: modern, premium, responsive, easy to manage and search-engine friendly. It will combine a polished public website with a secure content-management system so Flawless Skin Care can continue growing its online presence without depending on a developer for routine updates.
The final system is expected to include a dynamic product catalogue, secure admin dashboard, product and image management, CEO profile, services, booking requests, reviews, contact tools, WhatsApp and social links, Limbe location information, SEO foundations, secure database architecture and deployment to a live domain.
